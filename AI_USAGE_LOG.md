|---|---|---|---|
| ChatGPT / Codex | Research and problem exploration | Skill exchange framing, feature ideas and design critique prompts | Selected the exchange problem, rejected Frutiger Aero, chose the Apple 2016–2017 direction and set the desktop-only navigation constraint. |

| ChatGPT / Codex | Debugging and testing | Suggestions for validation, accessibility checks and browser QA | Ran the application, exercised routes and forms, inspected the rendered UI, measured contrast, verified image loading and ran the project test suites. |
| ChatGPT / Codex | Documentation support | Judge walkthrough, design rationale and QA report drafts | Edited the wording, recorded actual evidence and disclosed prototype limits, fictional profiles and AI assistance. |

## What was not delegated

The product concept, final visual direction, desktop-only navigation decision, information architecture, image variety requirement, user-flow decisions, final copy choices, critique judgments and verification decisions were made by the student. AI output was treated as a draft or tool-assisted material and was reviewed before inclusion.

## Demonstration promise

The student can explain `isMutual`, `findCircles`, `addRequest`, `validateStore`, route parsing, local persistence, native dialog behavior, the CSS motion system and the reason every major page exists. No AI-generated content is presented as entirely independent student work.
